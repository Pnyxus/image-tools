    How do I know nothing is uploaded?

    • Disconnect from the internet after the page opens — it keeps working normally.
    • Install it as an app: it opens and works even with no connection at all.
    • The “network requests” counter at the top shows the page sends nothing.
    • The page’s security policy (CSP) forbids the browser from connecting to any server.
    • Re-encoding strips EXIF metadata, including your photo’s GPS location.
